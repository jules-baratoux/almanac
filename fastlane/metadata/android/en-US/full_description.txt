Almanac is your friendly guide to popular events, festivals, and holidays throughout the year. Stay informed about what's coming up and feel ready to join every celebration.

WHAT ALMANAC DOES

Almanac gathers notable holidays and cultural celebrations into a single, easy-to-read timeline. Events are neatly organized into clear sections — Today, This Week, Next Week, This Month, and beyond — so you always know what's on the horizon.

FOR EACH EVENT YOU'LL FIND

• The date or date range
• A warm, clear description of what the event celebrates and its origin
• Practical tips on what to wear and what to bring
• Suggestions for traditional food and drink
• The greetings and phrases to say, plus simple etiquette

CELEBRATIONS FROM AROUND THE WORLD

From the Mid-Autumn Festival and National Day Golden Week to Halloween, Beaujolais Nouveau, Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Eve, Almanac highlights celebrations across many cultures, including traditions from the USA, China, France, Italy, and Mexico.

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT

• Clean, distraction-free reading experience
• Smart time-based grouping so upcoming events are always front and center
• Helpful, action-oriented tips to help you take part with confidence
• A simple way to learn about traditions from different cultures

Whether you're planning ahead, curious about world traditions, or simply want to make the most of every occasion, Almanac helps you stay in the know and celebrate with confidence.
